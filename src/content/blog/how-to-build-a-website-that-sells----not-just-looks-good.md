## Designing for Conversion

A website should be more than an aesthetic showcase; it is the ultimate digital vehicle for converting curiosity into high-intent client partnerships.

## Core Conversion Principles

1. **Clear visual hierarchy**: Eliminate decision fatigue with purposeful typography scales and generous white space.
2. **Speed as a feature**: Static generation with Astro eliminates client payload latency.
3. **Intentional micro-interactions**: Every animation should reinforce spatial consistency and user feedback.
